Bridal questions

Still unsure? Ask us anything on WhatsApp.

Which events do you do bridal makeup for?

Dulhan (bridal) makeup and hair for Nikkah, Mehndi, Baraat (also written Barat) and Walima. See our bridal services.

How do I book my bridal date?

On WhatsApp. Send your dates, a photo of each outfit and the jewellery you’ll wear, and we reply with availability, bridal options and prices. Your date is confirmed with us in that chat. The booking form writes the message for you.

Will the makeup match my skin tone?

That’s what we aim for. We match the base to your skin and never use a lighter shade. Tell us about any skin sensitivities when you message us.

Can I book hair, nails or skin care before the wedding?

Pre-bridal care covers hair, facial and nail appointments before the wedding. Ask about it on WhatsApp, or see all salon services.
