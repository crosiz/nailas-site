      Good to know

      Still unsure? Ask us anything on WhatsApp.

      How do I book?

      On WhatsApp. Send the service you’d like, your preferred date and, if you have one, a reference photo. We reply with availability, options and pricing, and your appointment is confirmed with us there. The booking form writes the message for you.

      Are the prices on the site final?

      They’re starting prices. The final price depends on the service and is confirmed on WhatsApp, together with availability, before you book.

      Do you do bridal makeup for every wedding event?

      Yes. We do makeup and hair for Nikkah, Mehndi, Baraat and Walima. Wedding season fills up early, so send us your dates as soon as you have them. See bridal makeup and hair.

      Do you teach courses?

      Yes. Naila’s Academy is the training side of the studio, with makeup, hairstyling and nail courses. Message us for the current options, or see beauty courses.
