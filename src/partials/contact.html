Good to know

Still unsure? Ask us anything on WhatsApp.

Can I book through the website?

The website doesn’t confirm bookings. Every booking button opens WhatsApp with a message ready to send, and your appointment is confirmed with the studio in that chat.

Can I bring a reference photo?

Yes. Bring a photo to your appointment or send it on WhatsApp beforehand, so we can plan the look together.

Should I mention sensitive skin?

Yes, please. Tell us about your skin, your routine and any sensitivities when you enquire, so we can suggest a suitable service.
