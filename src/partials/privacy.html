Privacynotice

What this website does and does not collect. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who we are

Naila’s Beauty Studio, A&K Plaza, Sangam Market, Sector I-8/3, Islamabad, Pakistan. You can reach us on WhatsApp or by phone at +92 333 5559049.

What this website collects

Nothing about you. It has no forms or accounts and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Our own pages set no cookies and load nothing from other companies, with one exception: the Google map on the Contact page (see below).

Hosting records

Like any website, the company that hosts it (Vercel Inc., USA) records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, the page you asked for and your browser type, so it can deliver the pages and keep them secure. We don’t use this to identify or follow visitors. See Vercel’s privacy policy.

The map on the Contact page

The Contact page shows a Google map. When that page loads, your browser connects to Google, which supplies the map and may set cookies or collect information about your visit under its own privacy policy. See Google’s privacy policy. No other page contains the map.

Links to other services

Our buttons open WhatsApp, your phone’s dialler, Instagram (@nailasbeautysalon) or Google Maps. When you tap one, that service learns that you came from our website and handles your information under its own privacy policy, not this one.

Booking on WhatsApp

Our WhatsApp buttons, such as “Send on WhatsApp” on the booking page, open WhatsApp with a message already typed for you, ending with a “Ref:” line that tells us which page or button you used. You can read and change the message before you send it, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

If you message us, we see your WhatsApp number, your name and what you write. We use them only to reply, arrange your appointment and follow it up. We keep chats for as long as we need them to look after your booking, and you can ask us to delete yours.

Photos

If you appear in a photo on this website and want it removed, message us on WhatsApp and we will take it down.

Children

This website does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or any other tool that collects information about visitors, we will update this page first and change the date at the top.
